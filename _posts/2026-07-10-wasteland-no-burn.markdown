# An Update from Death Guild Thunderdome

After 25 years on playa (and two years off), Death Guild Thunderdome will be taking a break from Burning Man this year. We will be at Wasteland Weekend 2026, and we look forward to returning to Burning Man in 2027. In the meantime, if you see one of us strolling aimlessly about the playa, please approach gently, with Bud Light and soothing words. 

We will miss you. *But our aim is getting better.*
