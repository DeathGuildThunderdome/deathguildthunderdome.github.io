# Wasteland Weekend 2026 Update

Welcome to another edition of Thunderdome! We have some big news: **starting this year, participants no longer need to sign up online ahead of time.**

## So what do you have to do instead?
* First, attend one of the mandatory safety training sessions hosted in the dome during the day, **bring your government-issued ID!!**
* Fill out and sign a legal form on-site at the end of the training.
* Hand your form to a crew member who will check your info against your ID and give you a participant wristband, good for the entire weekend. 

> **THEN AND ONLY THEN** will you be able to come back at night to be a part of the carnage. Thunderdome matches will start Wednesday night this year!

* **Wednesday** at 6pm
* **Thursday** at 5pm
